# Safety of Toys — Indian Standard IS 9873 Series

## Overview
IS 9873 is the Indian Standard series covering the safety requirements for toys sold in India. It is modeled conceptually on international toy-safety frameworks and is split into multiple parts covering different hazard categories.

## Applicability
Any toy intended for use by children under 14 years of age, manufactured in or imported into India, falls under the scope of this standard. This includes soft toys, plastic toys, battery-operated toys, and ride-on toys.

## Key Parts
- **Part 1 — Mechanical and Physical Properties**: Covers choking hazards from small parts, sharp edges, sharp points, and structural integrity (toys must not break into hazardous fragments under normal use or foreseeable abuse).
- **Part 2 — Flammability**: Specifies limits on how toy materials may burn, to reduce fire risk.
- **Part 3 — Migration of Certain Elements**: Sets maximum limits for migration of toxic elements (such as lead, cadmium, mercury, arsenic) from toy materials, relevant for painted or coated toys.
- **Part 9 — Chemical/Phthalate requirements**: Relevant for PVC/soft plastic toys, restricting certain plasticizer chemicals.

## Certification Requirement
Under the Toys (Quality Control) Order, toys covered by IS 9873 require **mandatory BIS certification** (ISI mark) before they can be legally manufactured, imported, or sold in India. This falls under the **Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)** framework administered by BIS.

## Certification Process (Simplified)
1. Manufacturer submits a license application to BIS along with product samples.
2. Samples are tested at a BIS-recognized laboratory against the applicable IS 9873 parts.
3. Factory inspection may be conducted to verify production consistency.
4. On successful testing and inspection, BIS grants a license permitting use of the ISI mark on the product.
5. Periodic surveillance testing continues after the license is granted to ensure ongoing compliance.

## Common Applicable Product Categories
- Soft/stuffed toys
- Plastic toys (rigid and PVC)
- Battery-operated electronic toys
- Ride-on toys and tricycles
- Puzzle and construction toys
