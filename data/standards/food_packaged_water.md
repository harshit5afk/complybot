# Packaged Drinking Water — Indian Standard IS 14543

## Overview
IS 14543 specifies the requirements for packaged (non-mineral) drinking water sold in bottles, pouches, or similar containers in India. This is distinct from IS 13428, which covers natural/packaged mineral water.

## Applicability
Any entity packaging and selling drinking water commercially — including bottled water brands, water dispensers selling packaged water, and institutional suppliers — falls under the scope of this standard.

## Key Requirements
- **Physical and chemical parameters**: pH, total dissolved solids, hardness, and limits on specific minerals/contaminants.
- **Microbiological requirements**: Limits on coliform bacteria, E. coli, and other pathogens — testing must confirm water is safe for direct human consumption.
- **Packaging requirements**: Food-grade packaging material, proper sealing, and batch/lot marking for traceability.
- **Labelling requirements**: Must display BIS license number, batch number, manufacturing and expiry/best-before dates, and net quantity.

## Certification Requirement
Packaged drinking water is covered under mandatory BIS product certification (ISI mark), administered through the Food and Agriculture department of BIS's certification framework — this is one of the standards where certification is legally mandatory before sale, due to direct public health relevance.

## Certification Process (Simplified)
1. Manufacturer applies to BIS with details of the manufacturing unit and water source.
2. Water samples are tested at a BIS-recognized lab for the full physical, chemical, and microbiological parameter list under IS 14543.
3. A factory audit is conducted covering hygiene, water treatment process, and packaging line.
4. On successful testing and audit, BIS grants the license to use the ISI mark.
5. Regular (often monthly or quarterly) surveillance sampling continues for the life of the license, given the public-health sensitivity of the product category.
