# Electrical Goods & LED Products — Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)

## Overview
Many electronic and electrical products sold in India — including LED lamps, power banks, mobile chargers, and similar consumer electronics — fall under the **Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS)** administered by BIS under the Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirements for Compulsory Registration) Order.

## Applicable Standards for LED Products
- **IS 16102 (Part 1)** — Self-ballasted LED lamps: performance requirements.
- **IS 16102 (Part 2)** — Self-ballasted LED lamps: safety requirements.
- **IS 16103** — LED modules for general lighting: safety requirements.

## Key Difference: ISI Mark vs CRS
- The **ISI Mark** (under product certification scheme) is typically used for products like toys, cement, and certain consumer goods, and involves a continuing license with periodic surveillance.
- **CRS registration** (used for most electronics/IT goods including LED lamps) is a **one-time registration** based on test reports from a BIS-recognized or BIS-approved lab, valid for the product model, renewed periodically (commonly every 2 years), without mandatory ongoing factory surveillance in the same way as ISI-marked products.

## Certification Process (CRS) — Simplified
1. Manufacturer gets the product tested at a BIS-recognized laboratory (or an approved lab in the country of manufacture, for imports).
2. Test report confirming compliance with the applicable IS standard (e.g., IS 16102) is submitted to BIS.
3. Manufacturer applies for registration through the BIS online CRS portal, declaring product details and the test report.
4. On verification, BIS grants a **registration number**, which must be displayed/marked on the product and its packaging (the "R-Number").
5. No ISI mark is used for CRS products — instead, the registration number and a standard CRS marking are displayed.

## Common Applicable Product Categories Under CRS
- LED lamps, LED drivers, and LED luminaires
- Mobile phone chargers and power adapters
- Power banks
- Certain IT equipment (laptops in some configurations, under separate provisions)

## Relevant Clause References (Illustrative)
- IS 16102 Part 1, Clause 6 — Photometric performance testing
- IS 16102 Part 2, Clause 9 — Electrical safety and insulation testing
